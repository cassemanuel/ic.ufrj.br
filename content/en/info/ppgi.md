## Overview and Contact

The **UFRJ Graduate Program in Informatics (PPGI)** offers **Master's and Doctoral degrees in Informatics**, jointly offered by the Institute of Computing (IC) and the Electronic Computing Center (NCE) at UFRJ.

**Academic Office:** {{< email "secretaria@ppgi.ufrj.br" >}}.

Visit the **[official PPGI portal](http://www.ppgi.ufrj.br/)** for faculty information, news and admission announcements.
